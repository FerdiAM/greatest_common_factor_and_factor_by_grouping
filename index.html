Multi-Variable Math Guide & Rules

Multi-Variable GCF

Find the greatest common factor for the numbers AND the lowest power for EACH variable present across all terms!

Example: Factor 12a²b³ - 18a³b² + 24ab²

• Numeric GCF = 6

• Lowest 'a' power = a¹ | Lowest 'b' power = b²

• Total GCF = 6ab²

• Answer: 6ab²(2a - 3a² + 4)

Grouping Across Variable Sets

Factor pairs by grouping even when terms use variables like m, n, p, q, u, v, x, y. Order of factors does not matter: (u + 3)(2v + 5) is equivalent to (2v + 5)(u + 3)!
